Managing your Founders Pack and subscriptions

How to manage anything you have bought from us.

What is in the Founders Pack

The Founders Pack is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It includes:

  • Beta access to Sundered Spire and all future closed betas
  • The full game at release
  • The exclusive Founder cosmetic set (cape, mount, banner)
  • 10,000 gems credited to your account
  • The Founder badge on your profile, permanently

How to cancel a subscription

  1. Sign in to your HeroCorp account.
  2. Go to Settings then Subscriptions.
  3. Click Manage on the subscription.
  4. Click "Cancel subscription".

You will keep access until the end of the current period. We do not do partial-month refunds, but we do not cut you off early either.

Cancelling on other platforms

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play Store, Profile, Payments and subscriptions
  • Steam: Account details, Subscriptions
