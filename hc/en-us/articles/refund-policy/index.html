Refund policy and how to request one

Our refund policy in plain English, plus how to request one.

What we refund

Unconsumed in-game currency. Refundable within 14 days. If you have spent some, we refund the unused portion at the same rate.

Accidental purchases. Refundable within 30 days as a one-time courtesy.

Game purchases. Sundered Spire purchases through Steam, Epic, or GOG follow that platform's refund policy - typically refundable within 14 days if you have played less than two hours.

Founders Pack. Refundable in full within 14 days if you have not used any of the included currency or claimed any cosmetic items.

What we cannot refund

  • Currency that has been consumed (spent on items, characters, or upgrades)
  • Subscriptions for completed billing periods
  • Items received as gifts from other players
  • Purchases made more than 30 days ago

Do not do a chargeback first. If you dispute a charge with your bank before contacting us, our system automatically locks the associated account. Write to us first.

How to request a refund

  1. Submit a support request.
  2. Select "Refund request" as the topic.
  3. Include the transaction ID from your purchase receipt.
  4. Tell us briefly why you are requesting the refund.

We respond to refund requests within 24 hours. The refund itself takes 3 to 10 business days to appear.
