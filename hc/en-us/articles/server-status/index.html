Checking server status and outages

If you cannot connect or sign in, the issue might be on our end.

Where to check

Our live status page shows every HeroCorp service in real time:

  • Account services (sign-in, profile)
  • Sundered Spire game servers (per region)
  • Pocket Forge cloud saves
  • Tinker Squad matchmaking
  • Web store and purchases

If a service is showing as down

You do not need to do anything - we are aware and working on it. There is no need to contact support for known outages.

If a service shows as up but you cannot connect

  1. Restart the game
  2. Check your internet connection
  3. Restart your router
  4. Try a different network
  5. Check whether your firewall or VPN is blocking the game

Compensation for outages

For significant outages affecting time-limited content, we typically compensate affected players with currency or extended event windows. Check your in-game mail.
