I cannot sign in to my account

If you cannot sign in to your HeroCorp account, work through these common causes in order.

1. Check your username and password

  • Caps Lock is not on
  • You are using your registered email, not your in-game display name
  • You are on the right HeroCorp account if you have multiple

2. Check your 2FA code

If you have two-factor authentication enabled and the 6-digit code is not working, the most common cause is your phone's clock being out of sync. Codes are time-based and rely on your device having the correct time.

3. Check for account lock

We temporarily lock accounts after too many failed sign-in attempts. The lock lifts automatically after 30 minutes.

4. Check your region

Some closed beta accounts are restricted to specific regions. Disable your VPN or contact support if you have permanently moved.

5. Check our server status

If everything on your end looks right, the issue might be on ours. Check our server status page for known outages.

If none of those worked

Submit a support request with:

  1. Your HeroCorp display name and registered email
  2. The exact error message you are seeing
  3. The platform you are trying to sign in from
  4. Whether 2FA is enabled on your account
