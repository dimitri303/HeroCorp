My gems or in-game currency have not arrived

If you bought gems, crystals, or other in-game currency and they have not shown up in your wallet, we can almost always fix it, usually in minutes.

Quick fix - try this first: fully close the game (not just minimise) and reopen it. About 70% of missing currency tickets are resolved by this single step.

If a restart did not fix it

Check whether the purchase actually completed on the payment provider's side. Open your email and look for a receipt from Steam, Apple, Google, PayPal, or whichever method you used.

If you have a receipt

Submit a support request with:

  1. Your HeroCorp display name
  2. The transaction ID from the receipt
  3. The currency amount you bought (for example, 5,000 gems)
  4. The approximate time of purchase

We can typically credit the missing currency within an hour during UK business hours, or by the next morning if you are writing overnight.

If you do not have a receipt

Check your bank or card statement to see whether the charge actually went through. If the charge did not appear, the purchase failed and no currency was deducted - just try again.

Common causes

  • Sync delay between platforms. Up to 15 minutes.
  • Payment held for review. First-time large purchases usually clear within an hour.
  • Wrong account. Make sure you are signed in to the account you purchased on.
  • Promotional bundles. Some release currency in tranches over several days.

If you bought currency for a time-limited event

If your purchase was specifically to participate in a time-limited event and it did not arrive in time, please mention this when you contact us. Where the missed window was caused by an issue on our end, we will either restore the relevant rewards or refund the currency.
