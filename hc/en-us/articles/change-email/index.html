Changing your email address or display name

You can update both the email address and the display name on your HeroCorp account from your account settings.

Changing your email address

  1. Sign in and go to Settings then Account.
  2. Click "Change email".
  3. Enter your new email address and your current password.
  4. Check both your old and new email inboxes - we send a confirmation link to both.
  5. Click the confirmation link in your new inbox to complete the change.

If you have lost access to your old email: follow the account recovery process. We will verify your identity another way and update your email manually.

Changing your display name

You can change your display name once every 30 days from Settings then Profile.

Display names must be:

  • Between 3 and 20 characters
  • Unique - if a name is taken, you will see an error
  • Compliant with our community guidelines

Founder display names

If you bought the Sundered Spire Founders Pack, your account is permanently flagged as a Founder regardless of how many times you change your display name.
