Sundered Spire closed beta access

The Sundered Spire closed beta is currently running.

How to get in

  1. Founders Pack. All Founders have permanent beta access.
  2. Wave invitations. We invite waitlisted players in batches.
  3. Streamer giveaways. We cannot reissue these - check the streamer's channel.

System requirements

Minimum: Windows 10/11, Intel i5-8400 or AMD equivalent, 8GB RAM, GTX 1060 or RX 580, 40GB storage.

Recommended: Windows 11, Intel i7-10700, 16GB RAM, RTX 3060 or RX 6600 XT, 60GB SSD storage.

Beta-specific things to know

  • Progress will be wiped at full release.
  • You will see bugs. Please report them.
  • Streaming and screenshots are allowed and encouraged.
  • Do not leak unannounced features found in game files.
