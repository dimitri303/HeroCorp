Setting up two-factor authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second layer of security to your HeroCorp account. Even if someone learns your password, they cannot sign in without the code from your authenticator app.

Before you start

You will need an authenticator app on your phone:

  • Google Authenticator (iOS / Android)
  • Authy (iOS / Android / desktop)
  • Microsoft Authenticator
  • 1Password or Bitwarden

Enabling 2FA

  1. Sign in to your HeroCorp account on the website or in-game.
  2. Go to Settings then Security.
  3. Click "Enable two-factor authentication".
  4. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
  5. Enter the 6-digit code from your app to confirm.
  6. Save your backup codes somewhere safe.

Backup codes matter. They are how you sign in if you lose your phone. Each code works once. If you lose both your phone and your backup codes, you will need to go through full account recovery.

Signing in with 2FA enabled

When you sign in you will be asked for a 6-digit code after entering your password. Open your authenticator app and enter the current code. Codes refresh every 30 seconds.

Lost your phone

Use one of your backup codes to sign in, then go to Settings then Security and reset 2FA with your new device. If you do not have your backup codes, follow the account recovery process.
