Billing questions and unrecognised charges

Seeing a charge on your statement that you do not recognise is unsettling. Here is how to figure out what it is for.

How HeroCorp charges appear

  • HEROCORP STUDIOS
  • HEROCORP GAMES
  • HEROCORP LTD UK
  • STRIPE HEROCORP (when payment is processed via Stripe)

If you bought through a platform like Steam or Apple, the charge will appear under that platform's name, not ours.

Step 1: Check who actually made the purchase

Common scenarios:

  • A child making in-game purchases on a shared account or device
  • A partner or family member buying on the same Apple Family or Google Family account
  • A subscription auto-renewal you forgot about
  • A pre-order or wishlisted item that is now released and charged

Step 2: Find the receipt

Search your email for HeroCorp. We send a receipt for every direct purchase.

Step 3: Check your account purchase history

Sign in to your HeroCorp account and go to Settings then Purchases.

If the charge is not in your purchase history

Contact us immediately and include:

  1. The exact name on the charge as it appears on your statement
  2. The amount and date
  3. The last four digits of the card that was charged

If it is in your history but you did not make it

Your account may have been compromised. Follow the account recovery process.
