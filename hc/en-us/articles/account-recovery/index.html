How to recover a locked or hacked account

If you cannot access your HeroCorp account - whether you have been locked out, suspect someone else has signed in, or noticed unfamiliar changes - we can help you recover it.

If your account was just compromised: submit a recovery request immediately. The faster you tell us, the easier it is to roll back unauthorised purchases or character changes. We can recover most accounts within 24 to 48 hours.

Situation 1: Forgotten password

Go to the sign-in screen and click "Forgot password?" - we will send a reset link to the email registered on the account. The link is valid for one hour.

Situation 2: Lost access to your email

If you can no longer sign in to the email address linked to your HeroCorp account, you will need to verify your identity through other means. Submit a recovery request through our support form and have ready:

  1. Your HeroCorp display name (in-game username)
  2. The approximate date you created the account
  3. One or more transaction IDs from past purchases (Steam, Apple, Google, or direct billing)
  4. A government-issued photo ID matching the name on file (if your account had real-name verification)

Situation 3: Account has been hacked

If you can still sign in, change your password immediately and enable two-factor authentication. Then sign out of all sessions from your account settings.

If you can no longer sign in, submit a recovery request and select "suspected unauthorised access". We will lock the account, investigate, and reverse any unauthorised purchases or in-game transactions made within the last 30 days.

What we can and cannot recover

We can usually recover: in-game currency spent without your authorisation, character cosmetics that were sold or transferred, deleted save files (within 90 days), and unauthorised real-money charges.

We cannot recover: consumed boost items, account progression on third-party platforms, or accounts that have been inactive for more than three years.

Preventing this in future

The single most important thing you can do is enable two-factor authentication. Accounts with 2FA enabled are over 99% less likely to be compromised.
