How to recover a locked or hacked account

Updated 02 May 2026

If you can't access your HeroCorp account — whether you've been locked out, suspect someone else has signed in, or noticed unfamiliar changes — we can help you recover it.

If your account was just compromised: submit a recovery request immediately. The faster you tell us, the easier it is to roll back unauthorised purchases. We recover most accounts within 24–48 hours.

Forgotten password

Go to the sign-in screen and click Forgot password? We'll send a reset link to the email on the account. The link is valid for one hour.

Lost access to your email

Submit a recovery request through our support form with: your HeroCorp display name, the approximate date you created the account, and one or more transaction IDs from past purchases.

Account has been hacked

If you can still sign in, change your password immediately and enable two-factor authentication, then sign out of all sessions. If you can't sign in, submit a recovery request and select "suspected unauthorised access". We'll lock the account, investigate, and reverse unauthorised purchases made within the last 30 days.

What we can recover

In-game currency spent without your authorisation, character cosmetics that were sold or transferred, deleted save files (within 90 days), and unauthorised real-money charges. We can't recover consumed boost items or accounts inactive for more than three years.
