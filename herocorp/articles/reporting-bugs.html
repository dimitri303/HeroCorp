How to report a bug

Updated 02 May 2026

We read every bug report. The ones we fix fastest are the ones we don't have to follow up on.

Where to report

Use the support form with topic "Bug report". Don't post bugs in Discord or social — they get lost.

What makes a good report

  1. What you were doing — describe the situation in plain English
  2. What you expected to happen
  3. What actually happened
  4. Whether it's reproducible — can you make it happen again on demand?
  5. Your system info — DxDiag file for PC, device model and OS version for mobile

Bonus: a screenshot or video

A 10-second clip of a visual glitch is worth pages of description. F12 takes a screenshot in Sundered Spire.

Severity matters

If the bug is destructive — loses progress, corrupts saves, blocks event completion, or costs currency — mark it urgent. We have a fast-track queue.

Will I hear back?

For severe or reproducible bugs, yes — usually within a working day. For minor visual issues, sometimes the bug just gets logged and fixed without a personal reply. Either way, your report gets read.
