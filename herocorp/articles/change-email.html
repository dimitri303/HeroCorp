Changing your email or display name

Updated 02 May 2026

You can update both your email address and display name from your account settings.

Changing your email address

  1. Sign in and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Click Change email.
  3. Enter your new email and current password.
  4. Check both old and new inboxes — we send a confirmation to both.
  5. Click the link in your new inbox to complete the change.
Lost access to your old email? You can't change your email through the standard process. Follow the account recovery process instead.

Changing your display name

You can change your display name once every 30 days from Settings → Profile. Names must be 3–20 characters, unique, and compliant with our community guidelines (no slurs, no staff impersonation).
