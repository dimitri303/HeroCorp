Your data, GDPR rights, and privacy

Updated 02 May 2026

We're a UK-based company and we take privacy seriously.

What we collect

Account info: email, display name, hashed password, creation date.

Purchase history: what you bought, when, through which platform. We don't store your card number — that's held by Stripe, Apple, or Google.

Gameplay data: progression, items, friends list, recent matches.

Technical data: platform, anonymised device info, crash logs.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell data to advertisers
  • We don't serve targeted ads in our games
  • We don't train AI on private player chat

Your rights under UK GDPR

  1. Access a copy of your data
  2. Rectify incorrect data
  3. Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
  4. Restrict processing
  5. Port your data elsewhere
  6. Object to specific uses

How to exercise these rights

Go to Settings → Data & Privacy to download an export, request deletion, or update preferences. If you can't access your account, send a privacy request through the support form.

Account deletion is permanent. After the 14-day cancellation window, we can't recover characters, items, or purchase history.

How long we keep data

Active accounts: as long as the account is active. Inactive 3+ years: anonymised. Deleted: personal data purged within 30 days. Some data retained for legal and tax reasons up to 7 years, detached from your identity.
