Billing questions and unrecognised charges

Updated 02 May 2026

Seeing a charge you don't recognise is unsettling. Here's how to figure out what it's for.

How HeroCorp charges appear

  • HEROCORP STUDIOS
  • HEROCORP*GAMES
  • HEROCORP LTD UK
  • STRIPE *HEROCORP

If you bought through Steam or Apple, the charge will appear under that platform's name.

Before assuming fraud

The most common explanation is someone in your household made a legitimate purchase: a child on a shared device, a partner on Apple Family, an auto-renewal you forgot, or a pre-order that's now released.

Find the receipt

Search your email for "HeroCorp". We send a receipt for every direct purchase. Then check Settings → Purchases in your account.

If the charge isn't in your purchase history

Contact us with: the exact name on the charge, amount, date, and last four digits of the card.

If it's in your history but you didn't make it

Your account may have been compromised. Follow the account recovery process. We can refund unauthorised charges within 30 days of purchase.
