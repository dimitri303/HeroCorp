My gems or in-game currency haven't arrived

Updated 02 May 2026

If you bought gems and they haven't shown up in your wallet, we can almost always fix it.

Quick fix — try this first: fully close the game and reopen it. About 70% of missing currency tickets are resolved by this single step.

If a restart didn't fix it

Check your email for a receipt from Steam, Apple, Google, PayPal, or whichever payment method you used.

If you have a receipt

Submit a support request with: your display name, the transaction ID from the receipt, the currency amount (e.g. "5,000 gems"), and the approximate time of purchase. We credit missing currency within an hour during UK business hours.

If you don't have a receipt

Check your bank statement to see if the charge went through. If the charge didn't appear, the purchase failed — just try again. If the charge did appear, contact us with as much info as you have.

Common causes

  • Sync delay between platforms. Up to 15 minutes.
  • Payment held for review. First-time large purchases usually clear within an hour.
  • Wrong account. Make sure you're signed in to the account you purchased on.

Time-limited events

If your purchase was for a time-limited event and didn't arrive in time, please mention this when you contact us. We'll restore the rewards or refund the currency — we'll make it right.
