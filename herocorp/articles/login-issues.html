I can't sign in to my account

Updated 02 May 2026

If you can't sign in, work through these in order — one of them is almost always the issue.

1. Check username and password

Caps Lock isn't on. You're using your registered email, not your display name. If you've forgotten your password, click "Forgot password?"

2. Check your 2FA code

If the 6-digit code isn't working, your phone's clock is probably out of sync. Enable automatic time on your phone.

3. Check for account lock

We temporarily lock accounts after too many failed attempts. The lock lifts automatically after 30 minutes.

4. Check your region

Some closed beta accounts are restricted to specific regions. Disable your VPN or contact support if you've permanently moved.

5. Check our server status

If everything on your end looks right, the issue might be on ours. Check our server status page for known outages.

If none of those worked

Submit a support request with: your display name and registered email, the exact error message, the platform you're using, and whether 2FA is enabled.
