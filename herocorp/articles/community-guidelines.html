Community guidelines and reporting players

Updated 02 May 2026

HeroCorp games are for everyone. These guidelines make that work.

What we don't allow

Harassment and hate speech

No slurs, no targeted harassment, no hate speech. Applies to chat, voice, display names, and clan names.

Cheating and exploiting

No third-party software for unfair advantage. No exploiting bugs for profit. No account selling or real-money trading.

Scams and impersonation

Don't pretend to be HeroCorp staff. We never DM you for your password.

Inappropriate content

No sexually explicit content. No gore. No extremist content.

Spam and disruption

Don't flood chat. Don't advertise unrelated services. Don't troll or throw matches.

How to report a player

Right-click their name in chat or on the post-match screen and select Report. Add a description and screenshot if you have one. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours for serious cases, 72 for routine.

What happens after a report

Action ranges from a warning to chat mute to suspension to permanent ban. You'll see "Action was taken on your report" in your inbox if it led to a sanction.

Appealing a ban

Submit a support request with topic "Account action appeal". Reviewed within 48 hours. Be honest — we have full chat logs.

If you're being threatened in real life: contact us with topic "Player safety". We escalate immediately and will work with law enforcement where appropriate.
