Refund policy and how to request one

Updated 02 May 2026

Our refund policy in plain English, plus how to request one.

What we refund

Unconsumed in-game currency. Refundable within 14 days. If you've spent some, we refund the unused portion at the same rate.

Accidental purchases. Refundable within 30 days as a one-time courtesy.

Game purchases. Sundered Spire purchases through Steam, Epic, or GOG follow that platform's policy — typically refundable within 14 days if you've played less than two hours.

Founders Pack. Refundable in full within 14 days if you haven't used the included currency or claimed cosmetic items.

What we can't refund

  • Currency that has been consumed
  • Subscriptions for completed billing periods
  • Items received as gifts
  • Purchases made more than 30 days ago
Don't do a chargeback first. If you dispute a charge with your bank before contacting us, our system automatically locks the account. Write to us first — we resolve almost all disputes within 24 hours.

How to request a refund

  1. Submit a support request.
  2. Select Refund request.
  3. Include the transaction ID.
  4. Tell us briefly why.

We respond within 24 hours during the working week. The refund takes 3–10 business days to appear.
