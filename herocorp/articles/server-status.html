Checking server status and outages

Updated 02 May 2026

If you can't connect or sign in, the issue might be on our end.

Where to check

Our live status page shows every HeroCorp service in real time: account services, Sundered Spire game servers, Pocket Forge cloud saves, Tinker Squad matchmaking, and the web store.

You can also follow @HeroCorpStatus on social.

If a service is down

You don't need to do anything — we're aware and working on it. There's no need to contact support for known outages.

If a service shows up but you can't connect

  1. Restart the game
  2. Check your internet
  3. Restart your router
  4. Try a different network
  5. Check whether your firewall or VPN is blocking the game

Compensation

For significant outages affecting time-limited content, we typically compensate affected players with currency or extended event windows. Check your in-game mail.
