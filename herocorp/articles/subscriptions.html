Managing your Founders Pack and subscriptions

Updated 02 May 2026

How to manage anything you've bought from us.

What's in the Founders Pack

The Founders Pack is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It includes: beta access to Sundered Spire, the full game at release, the exclusive Founder cosmetic set, 10,000 gems, and the permanent Founder badge.

There's nothing to cancel or renew. For refunds within the eligibility window, see our refund policy.

Cancelling a subscription

  1. Sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings → Subscriptions.
  3. Click Manage on the subscription.
  4. Click Cancel subscription.

You'll keep access until the end of the current period. We don't do partial-month refunds, but we don't cut you off early either.

Cancelling on other platforms

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions
  • Steam: Account details → Subscriptions
